ZERO HOUR: Mobile Skirmish

USA vs GLA  ·  single-file RTS  ·  all-SVG assets

  • Tap a unit/building to select it. Double-tap selects all of that type on screen.
  • Tap ground to move · tap an enemy to attack.
  • Drag to pan the map · pinch to zoom · tap the minimap to jump.
  • ⬚ toggles drag-box multi-select · ⚔ selects your whole army.
  • Select the Dozer to construct buildings. Build a Supply Center — its Chinook truck gathers supply crates for cash.
  • Trucks loop gathering automatically. Trucks & dozers repair: tap a damaged building, or they fix nearby structures when idle. Dozers resume unfinished construction.
  • ⟳ (or the R key) restarts the match.
  • 1·2·3 control groups: tap = select, long-press = assign, tap again = jump to them. Units earn veterancy chevrons from kills.
  • Weapons matter: guns shred infantry, rockets crack armor & buildings, cannons are all-round.
  • Keep ⚡ power positive or production slows and defenses go offline.
  • Fog of war hides the enemy — send a scout to reveal the map. Explored ground stays on your minimap.

Mission: the GLA holds the north-east. Destroy every enemy structure. They will send raids — dig in fast, General.
