Why everyone keeps coming back

Not a chain. A living room
that happens to serve coffee.

Crafted, never rushed

Specialty espresso, teas, hot chocolate, 100% fruit smoothies and specialty sodas — pulled and poured by baristas who know your order.

Sourced down the street

Reed’s Dairy ice cream, house-made breakfast sandwiches, local muffins, and cake pops & cheesecakes from family-run Paisley Cakes.

A little whimsy

Eclectic furniture, rotating local art, festive seasonal decor and a famous Harry Potter birthday bash every July 31st.

Owned with heart

June Lockhart opened the doors in 2018 and still greets regulars by name, working right alongside her baristas.
